About Me

The story behind the screen

A curious builder who merges logic with aesthetics.

I'm Trilochan Upadhyay, a BSc. CSIT student at Amrit Science Campus, deeply passionate about the intersection of technology and design. I believe the best digital experiences are born when engineering precision meets creative intuition.

My journey spans programming, graphic design, branding, UI/UX, typography, and digital content creation. I bring ideas to life using tools like Canva and am constantly expanding into code-driven design.

I'm also deeply curious about Artificial Intelligence and AI ethics — exploring how these technologies shape society and how we can build them responsibly. I learn best by doing: hands-on projects, experimentation, and iteration.
