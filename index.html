About me
I’m a designer who codes, a developer who draws, and a student trying to survive — assignments.

Currently pursuing BSc. CSIT at Amrit Science Campus, I live at the intersection of technology and creativity. I believe the most memorable digital products aren’t just functional — they feel intentional, warm, and human.

My craft spans graphic design, branding, UI/UX, and typography. I use Canva as my daily design weapon and am constantly pushing deeper into code. Each project is a new experiment.

Graphic Design UI/UX Branding Typography

I’m also deeply curious about Artificial Intelligence and AI ethics — how these systems shape society, whose values they encode, and how we build them responsibly.

I learn best by doing. And also I make too many mistakes. 🚀
